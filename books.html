Books That Changed My Life

August 2026

It can be a tricky thing to evaluate the quality of a book. A book depends on the reader, and the stage of life the reader is in. There are many books that I thought were shockingly deep in my early twenties, but do not hold up with more life experience. There is something about being exposed to an idea for the first time, or getting a template for a situation you don't have personal life experience with. I would imagine that many "business books" have a short shelf life. Many such books I now find to be trite eye rollers... "you mean to succeed in business I need to pay attention to detail and stay motivated?"

But nevertheless, here are some books that changed the way I thought about things. Click a book to read why.
