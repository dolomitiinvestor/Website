Books That Changed My Life

August 2026

It can be a tricky thing to evaluate the quality of a book. A book depends on the reader, and the context in which you read it. There are many books that I thought were shockingly deep in my teens and early 20s, but it is possible that they do not hold up with more life experience. There is something about being exposed to an idea for the first time, or getting a template for a situation you don't have personal life experience with. I would imagine that many "business books" have a short shelf life. Many such books I now find to be trite eye rollers... "you mean to succeed in business I need to pay attention to detail and stay motivated?"

But nethertheless, here are some books that changed the way I thought about things: -The Power Broker - Caro -Endurance - Lansing -From Third World to First - Yew -Hackers & Painters - Graham -The Design of Everyday Things - Norman -Never Split the Difference - Voss -The Richest Man in Bablyson - Clason -How to Win Friends & Influence People - Carnegie -The First 90 Days - Watkins -First Break All the Rules - Buckingham -What it takes - Schwarzman

Other books that were very good but perhaps not lifechanging:
With the Old Breed - Sledge Conquistador Voices - Siepel Berlin Diary - Shirer The Making of the Atomic Bomb - Rhodes Surely You're Joking Mr. Feynman! - Feynman On Writing Well - Zinsser Being Mortal - Gawande The Signal and the Noise - Silver Empire of the Summer Moon - Gwynne Skunk Works - Rich Career Warfare - D'Alessandro Debt - Graeber Sustainable Energy - MacKay Creativity Inc - Catmull Jobs - Isaacson Extreme Ownership - Willink Guadacanal Diary - Tregaskis Man's Search for Meaning - Frankl Shoe Dog - Knight The Goal - Goldratt Zero to One - Thiel Gulag Archipelago - Solzheneitsyn Into Thin Air - Krakauer For Whom the Bell Tolls - Hemingway Ego is the Enemy - Holiday Deep Work - Newport
