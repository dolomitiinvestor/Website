AI Writing Policy

August 2026

No AI is used to write posts. Full stop.

I don't generate entire paragraphs with AI, I don't rewrite shorthand with AI, I don't use sentence complete, and I don't use AI to generate ideas. If any AI whatsoever is involved in the creation of an article, it will be disclosed at the top of the post. For instance, from time to time I will have a back and forth with a contrarian AI to steelman an idea and explore the problem space.

In a world where AI is ubiquitous and cheap, the value of AI writing is zero. If someone asks an AI "what do you think about X", and then reposts the output, anyone else has access to very similar information. If the output is differentiated, the value would be in posting the prompt itself - the only human element.

I think there will always be a space for human writing. Either due to actual value or sentimental value. Similar to how people value craft and will buy rough-hewn handmade objects at a market. An artisinal vase is "worse" than a mass produced one, but it feels better to some consumers. For actual utilitarian value, the long-term picture is still unclear. It is possible the AI will get really good at writing. It is also possible the writing will stay too generic and will never be able to come up with a great marketing hook. Art is distinctive and you can at least argue has a uniquely human element to it. But business writing and other forms that still require some taste seem like they could be hacked. We are already seeing slop bots run writing through a secondary tool that takes out the "claude tells", or seeing humans paraphrase AI to rewrite outputs in a more imperfect shorthand.

Sincerely yours, Dolomiti
