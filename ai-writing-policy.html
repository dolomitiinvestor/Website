AI Writing Policy

August 2026

No AI is used to write posts. Full stop.

I don't generate paragraphs, I don't rewrite with AI, I don't use sentence complete, I don't use AI to generate ideas. If any AI whatsoever is involved, it will be disclosed at the top of the post.

In a world where AI is ubiquitous and cheap, the value of AI writing is 0. I think there will always be a space for human writing for enjoyment. Similar to how people value craft and will buy rough hewn handmade objects at a farmers market.

Sincerely yours, Dolomiti
