AI Writing Policy

August 2026

No AI is used to write posts. Full stop.

I don't generate entire paragraphs with AI, I don't rewrite shorthand with AI, I don't use sentence complete, and I don't use AI to generate ideas. If any AI whatsoever is involved, it will be disclosed at the top of the post. One example might be a back and forth with a contrarian AI to steelman an idea and explore the problem space.

In a world where AI is ubiquitous and cheap, the value of AI writing is zero. I think there will always be a space for human writing. Either due to actual value or sentimental value. Similar to how people value craft and will buy rough hewn handmade objects at a farmers market. An artisinal vase is "worse" than a mass produced one, but it feels better to some consumers. For actual value, the long term picture is unclear, but it is possible that AI generates writing too generic and wouldn't make a great marketing hook.

Sincerely yours, Dolomiti
